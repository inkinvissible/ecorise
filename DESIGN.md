# Ecorise Design System

## Design intent

Ecorise should feel like an engineering and energy advisory firm with commercial maturity: precise, composed and contemporary. Avoid the generic renewable-energy-template look, excessive green iconography, floating cards, gratuitous gradients and salesy solar clichés.

## Visual world

- Base: warm white and very pale mineral surfaces.
- Ink: deep desaturated charcoal-green.
- Brand accent: Ecorise teal, used with restraint for actions and emphasis.
- Photography: real infrastructure, panels, industrial/agricultural environments and existing Ecorise assets. Avoid decorative stock imagery that does not describe the service.
- Layout: editorial, broad whitespace, asymmetric content blocks and larger text measures. Not a dashboard grid.

## Palette

- `--ec-primary: #258b82`
- `--ec-primary-dark: #176760`
- `--ec-ink: #172425`
- `--ec-muted: #5f6d6c`
- `--ec-surface: #f4f7f5`
- `--ec-line: #dce5e2`
- `--ec-accent: #d9ede8`

## Typography

- Display and headings: Montserrat, with controlled negative tracking, max display size below 6rem.
- Body: Cabin, 65–75ch where possible.
- Hierarchy comes from scale, whitespace and weight, not all-caps labels above every heading.

## Components

- Navigation: white, compact, business-first labels, visible keyboard focus.
- Hero: one clear proposition, one primary CTA and at most one secondary action. Strong photography with a dark readable overlay.
- Cards: use only when content truly benefits from grouping. Prefer editorial split layouts, timelines and image/text compositions.
- CTA surfaces: solid dark/teal surfaces. No decorative glassmorphism.
- Buttons: modest radius, strong contrast, clear hover and focus states.
- Browser details: branded focus ring, selection color and link underline behavior.

## Motion

Use motion sparingly. Prefer one intentional reveal or image movement rather than repeated entrance animations on every section. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop layouts may use asymmetric columns. On mobile, content becomes a single readable flow, primary CTA expands when helpful, navigation remains compact and no horizontal overflow is acceptable.

## Content rules

- Never fabricate savings, ROI, payback, clients or project outcomes.
- Avoid positioning Ecorise as merely a solar-panel installer.
- Explain technical decisions in business language without losing engineering credibility.
- Sustainability language can appear when relevant, but the current core narrative is energy strategy, solar, engineering, operation and performance.
