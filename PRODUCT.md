# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience: owners, directors, plant managers and operational decision-makers at SMEs and mid-sized companies in Córdoba and Argentina evaluating energy investments, especially solar, efficiency, continuity and related engineering decisions.

Secondary audience: residential visitors remain part of the institutional surface, but the strategic priority of the site is business energy decision-making.

## Product Purpose

Ecorise helps companies understand, model and execute energy decisions with technical and financial criteria. The website must build credibility, explain complex energy choices clearly, qualify opportunities and move serious prospects toward an executive diagnosis rather than behave as a generic solar installer brochure.

## Positioning

Ecorise is positioned as a technical energy partner that starts with the operation and the business case, then defines the appropriate technology. Solar is a solution inside the strategy, not the whole proposition.

## Operating Context

Visitors often arrive from paid advertising and compare Ecorise with installers or equipment sellers. The site therefore needs to explain why diagnosis, modeling, engineering and follow-up matter before a capital decision. High-intent actions include scheduling a diagnosis, using the preliminary energy assessment and contacting the team.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript site hosted from the repository.
- Existing brand assets and photography should be reused when truthful and suitable.
- Existing Google Ads / Analytics / Meta tracking must not be casually removed.
- Do not fabricate savings, payback periods, certifications, clients, case studies or performance numbers.
- The preliminary calculator is exploratory and must never look like a guaranteed quote or engineering study.
- The current strategic focus is energy solutions and engineering; sustainability/carbon-footprint messaging must not dominate the site.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ecorise.
- Existing turquoise/teal identity remains recognizable.
- Tone: technically credible, clear, business-oriented, calm and premium rather than flashy or sales-heavy.
- Córdoba, Argentina remains the geographic anchor while communication can support work beyond the city.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing Ecorise logo and photography in `img/`.
- Existing pages for solar energy, technical consulting and maintenance.
- No validated case studies, hard savings claims or payback claims have been supplied for this redesign. Future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Understand the operation before proposing technology.
2. Make technical complexity legible to business decision-makers.
3. Qualify serious opportunities instead of maximizing low-intent clicks.
4. Prefer credible evidence and transparent assumptions over aggressive claims.
5. Treat the site as one coherent Ecorise experience, not separate institutional and B2B visual products.
